## English (for stores that ask)

**Title:** Dozari — Iranian Price Nostalgia

**Short:** Sort 16 old Iranian prices into 4 hidden groups. Does the penny drop?

**Description:**

Remember what a soda cost? An ice cream? A bus ticket? One coin for a payphone call?

Dozari is a puzzle game built on Iran's old prices. You get 16 products and have to sort them into 4 hidden groups, each with a playful rule: same price, same year, same family. The moment you crack one is the moment "the penny drops" (that's the name: *dozari* was the old two-rial coin).

• Live 1v1 and 2v2 matches, plus private tables with an invite code
• Daily puzzle, lucky wheel, tournaments and leaderboards
• Levels: puzzles get harder as you improve
• A bazaar-town hub, tribes by city, and a fitting room for your character
• Real historical nominal prices (never inflation-adjusted) with product images and a price chart after every match
• No Google services required; small and fast

The app is in Persian (right-to-left).
